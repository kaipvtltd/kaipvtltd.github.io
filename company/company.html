Core Objectives

  1. Biomass Processing: To carry on the business of processing biomass in all its forms, including but not limited to manufacturing, processing, production, generation, conversion, distribution, sale, and supply of biomass-based renewable energy, power, fuel, and allied products such as biocoal pellets, briquettes, biogas, ethanol, biodiesel, biomass pulp, animal fodder, bio-fertilizers, and other bio-energy and bio-based products.
  2. Agri-Value Addition: To engage in the production, processing, and value addition of agricultural produce, including but not limited to tilhan, dalhan, edible seeds, honey, milk and dairy products, oils, and fats.
  3. Waste-to-Energy: To establish and operate a sustainable pyrolysis-based system for processing various waste materials into renewable energy and valuable by-products for commercial distribution.
