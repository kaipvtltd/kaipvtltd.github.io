To minimize the footprint for a dual 2 TPH (4 TPH total) plant, we shift from a "sprawling" layout to a **Vertical & Integrated Design**. By using shared infrastructure and high-density storage, you can compress the requirement to approximately **1.2 to 1.5 acres** (down from the typical 3+ acres).

### 1. The "Lean" Floor Plan (Shed Layout)

The key to saving space is a **U-shaped or Parallel flow** where the two lines share a central "spine" for utilities and finished goods.

**Total Shed Size:** Approx. **180 ft x 120 ft (21,600 sq. ft.)**

### 2. Space-Saving Strategies (The "Cheat Sheet")

* **Shared Heat Source:** Instead of two furnaces, install **one large 5-6 million kcal furnace**. It sits in the center and pipes heat to both dryers. This saves ~1,000 sq. ft.
* **Vertical Storage (Silos):** Instead of storing ground powder on the floor, use **vertical buffer bins (silos)** between the hammer mill and the pellet press. This uses height (Z-axis) rather than floor space (X/Y-axis).
* **The "Mezzanine" Setup:** Place the cyclones and dust collection systems on a heavy-duty steel mezzanine floor *above* the pellet mills.
* **Integrated Cooling:** Use a single, high-capacity **Counter-flow Cooler** at the end. Pellets from both lines can enter the same cooler (if the feedstock is being blended) or a partitioned cooler to save 400 sq. ft.
* **Live-Floor Receiving:** For the agro-line, use a "Live Floor" (walking floor) bunker. It allows you to pile material 15 feet high rather than spreading it across the yard.

---

### 3. Critical Trade-off: The "Fire Buffer"

While we are minimizing space, you **cannot** compromise on the 6-meter (20 ft) "Fire Road" around the shed. Insurance companies and local fire departments require this for industrial permits.

### Important Misconception Note

Be careful with **Agro-residue moisture**. Many people try to skip the dryer to save space. However, if your feedstock is $>15\%$ moisture, skipping the dryer will lead to frequent machine jams and low-quality bio-coal. In a compact plant, a **Flash Dryer** is a great space-saving alternative to a Long Rotary Drum.
