Setting up a **2 TPH (tons per hour)** bio-coal (biomass pellet or torrefied pellet) plant requires a significant footprint, primarily because of the volume of raw material (biomass) that must be stored to ensure continuous operation.

On average, a 2 TPH plant requires **1.5 to 2 acres** of total land.

### 1. Land Area Breakdown

The space is typically divided into four main zones. For a 2 TPH capacity, the built-up area alone usually covers **25,000–30,000 sq. ft.**

| Component                  | Space Requirement (Approx.) | Purpose                                                                           |
| ---                        | ---                         | ---                                                                               |
| **Raw Material Yard**      | 10,000 – 15,000 sq. ft.     | Open or semi-covered area for incoming bulky biomass (husks, stalks, wood).       |
| **Processing Shed**        | 12,000 – 15,000 sq. ft.     | Covered area for the hammer mill, dryer, pellet/briquette press, and conveyors.   |
| **Finished Goods Storage** | 5,000 – 7,000 sq. ft.       | Dry, moisture-proof storage for finished bio-coal bags or bulk silos.             |
| **Utilities & Logistics**  | 3,000 – 5,000 sq. ft.       | Weighbridge, electrical room (transformer/DG set), office, and truck maneuvering. |

---

### 2. Space Variations by Feedstock

The type of biomass significantly impacts your storage needs due to **bulk density**.

* **High-Density Feedstock (Sawdust, Coffee Husks, Groundnut Shells):** These are compact and flow easily. You can get away with the lower end of the space estimates (~1.5 acres).
* **Low-Density/Bulky Feedstock (Paddy Straw, Cotton Stalks, Sugarcane Bagasse):** These require massive storage yards because 1 ton of loose straw occupies much more volume than 1 ton of sawdust. If using straw, you may need closer to **2.5 acres** to maintain a 15–30 day inventory.

---

### 3. Key Infrastructure Requirements

* **Shed Height:** The processing shed needs a minimum height of **20–25 feet**. This is necessary to accommodate vertical equipment like cyclones, bucket elevators, and large dryers.
* **Power Infrastructure:** A 2 TPH plant typically has a connected load of **350 kW**. You will need space for a dedicated transformer and a 250 kVA backup Generator (DG set).
* **Water Access:** You’ll need roughly **5,000–8,000 liters/day** for the cooling systems and dust suppression (misting).
* **Logistics:** Ensure a wide entry gate and turning radius for 10–15 ton trucks. A **30–50 ton weighbridge** is standard for a plant of this scale.

### 4. Technical Workflow & Equipment

The space must be laid out linearly to ensure efficient material flow:

1. **Sizing:** Hammer mills or chippers to reduce material to 3–5mm.
2. **Drying:** Rotary drum dryers (essential if feedstock moisture is $>15\%$).
3. **Densification:** The pellet mill or briquetting press.
4. **Cooling & Screening:** Bio-coal must be cooled to ambient temperature before bagging to prevent "sweating" and mold.
