Reducing the scale from **2 TPH (48 Tons Per Day)** to **4 TPD** (Tons Per Day) is a massive shift. You are moving from a large industrial setup to what is essentially a **Micro-Plant** or "Cottage Industry" scale.

At 4 TPD, the machinery is smaller, the power requirements are lower, and the logistics change from heavy trucks to small tractor-trolleys or pickups. You can fit this entire operation into a space roughly the size of a **large residential backyard or a small warehouse (approx. 4,000–6,000 sq. ft.)**.

### 1. The "Micro-Plant" Compact Layout

For 4 TPD, you typically use a **Flat Die Pellet Mill** rather than the large "Ring Die" mills used in TPH plants. These are much more compact.

**Minimum Shed Size:** 40 ft x 30 ft (1,200 sq. ft.)
**Total Land Needed:** ~5,000 sq. ft. (0.11 Acres)

### 2. Space-Saving Strategies for 4 TPD

At this micro-scale, you can replace expensive, bulky machinery with manual or natural processes:

* **Solar Drying:** Instead of a mechanical Flash Dryer (which is huge), you can spread the feedstock on a **cemented floor** or raised mesh trays. This uses 0 energy and saves the space of a furnace and drum.
* **Vertical "Gravity" Feed:** Place your small hammer mill on a 4-foot platform. The ground powder falls directly into the mixer or pellet mill hopper, eliminating the need for a 15-foot conveyor belt.
* **Manual Cooling:** Instead of a mechanical counter-flow cooler, spread the hot pellets on a **perforated steel rack**. At 4 TPD (about 500kg per 3-hour run), natural air cooling is sufficient.
* **Jumbo Bag Storage:** Instead of a dedicated warehouse, use UV-protected jumbo bags. These can be stacked in a corner, requiring very little square footage.

### 4. Resource Requirements

* **Power:** 25–40 HP (approx. 20–30 kW). This can often run on a standard commercial three-phase connection without needing a private transformer.
* **Labor:** 2–3 persons (one for feeding, one for monitoring, one for bagging).
* **Water:** Minimal (only for occasional moisture adjustment in the mixer).

### 5. Why the "4 TPD" Model is Popular

This scale is ideal for **decentralized production**. Instead of bringing biomass to a giant plant, you put a 4 TPD plant at the source (e.g., inside a furniture cluster or a large farm).

**The Footprint Advantage:** Since the raw material is used almost immediately, you don't need the massive 10,000 sq. ft. storage yard required for TPH plants. You only need enough space for about **8–10 tons of inventory**.
