Manufacturing Process Flow

Agri-Residue Pellet Production Sequence

01 Raw Material Collection
Gathering paddy straw, wheat straw, rice husk, or mustard stalk directly from fields and local mills during respective harvesting seasons.
↓
02 Baling & Transportation
Compressing loose crop residues into dense, manageable bales using field balers to optimize transit capacity and logistic costs.
↓
03 Storage Yard
Strategic stockpiling of secured raw materials in a designated storage depot to ensure continuous, year-round manufacturing operations.
↓
04 Shredding
Primary mechanical breaking down of large bales and long stalks into coarse, uniform particle lengths.
↓
05 Grinding
Secondary milling to pulverize rough shredded fibers into a fine, consistent powdery mash ideal for extrusion.
↓
06 Drying (If Required)
Thermal conditioning using a drum or flash dryer to optimize moisture levels, ensuring optimal cohesion and preventing structural defects.
↓
07 Pelletizing
Extruding the conditioned raw mash through high-pressure pellet dies where friction, heat, and natural binders fuse the material into solid cylindrical pellets.
↓
08 Cooling
Passing newly formed hot pellets through a counter-flow cooler to lower structural temperatures to ambient levels, hardening and stabilizing them.
↓
09 Screening
Sifting the finished product over vibratory meshes to filter out loose fines and broken fragments, routing dust back for re-pelletization.
↓
10 Bagging / Bulk Loading
Packing high-quality pellets into commercial storage bags or directly feeding bulk distribution trucks via overhead hopper chutes.
↓
11 Dispatch
Logistics coordinating outfeed delivery of prepared pellet batches to consumer markets or regional commercial energy clients.
