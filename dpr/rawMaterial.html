Key Takeaways

  • Cheapest Options (₹2/kg): Direct field collection of Paddy Straw and Wheat Straw yields the lowest cost, though it requires managing equipment and labor.
  • Convenience Premium (₹3/kg): Buying from third-party providers eliminates operational hassle for Paddy, Wheat, and Mustard stalks but increases the raw material cost by up to 50%.
  • Peak Supply Window: Material availability is highly seasonal, spanning from November through May, leaving a critical gap in the monsoon and late summer months (June – October) that may require stockpiling.
