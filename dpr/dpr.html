2. What are Bio coal Pellets, Project Background and Project Rationale
Particular Detail
Bio Coal Pellets Bio coal pellet is a renewable, green, eco-friendly, solid, cylindrical shaped fuel produced by compressing biomass like agricultural and forestry waste into high-density, energy-concentrated pellets under high pressure. The natural lignin present in biomass acts as a binder eliminating the need for chemical additives. It is a replacement for thermal coal and can be used by industries that burn thermal coal for heat.
Project Goal This project aims to establish a modern Bio Coal manufacturing plant utilizing locally available biomass such as Paddy Husk, Paddy Straw, Wheat Straw and Mustard Stalk to produce Non-Torrefied Bio Coal Pellets- a sustainable alternative to thermal coal for near by Power Plants (NTPC Nabinagar, SJVN Chausa) and other industries (Dalmia Cement Plant Banjari Rohtas), etc.
