2. Introduction to Bio coal Pellets, Project Goal, Background and Rationale
Particular Detail
Bio Coal Pellets Bio coal pellet is a renewable, green, eco-friendly, solid, cylindrical shaped fuel produced by compressing biomass like agricultural and forestry waste into high-density, energy-concentrated pellets under high pressure. The natural lignin present in biomass acts as a binder eliminating the need for chemical additives. It is a replacement for thermal coal and can be used by industries that burn thermal coal for heat.
Project Goal This project aims to establish a modern, scalable, reliable Bio Coal manufacturing plant utilizing locally available biomass such as Paddy Husk, Paddy Straw, Wheat Straw and Mustard Stalk to produce Non-Torrefied Bio Coal Pellets- a sustainable alternative to thermal coal for near by Power Plants (NTPC Nabinagar, SJVN Chausa) and other industries (Dalmia Cement Plant Banjari Rohtas), etc.
Why Agri Residue Based While bio-coal pellets can be manufactured from wood, utilizing agricultural residue is a far more sustainable and locally abundant alternative. Our region generates vast quantities of crop waste—primarily paddy husk, paddy straw, wheat straw, and mustard stalk—a significant portion of which is traditionally eliminated through open field burning. In line with the Government of India’s aggressive renewable energy initiatives promoting agricultural waste utilization, converting this residue into bio-coal pellets turns a massive environmental challenge into a strategic economic opportunity. This model creates a circular economy that mitigates air pollution, provides farmers with a reliable secondary income stream, and directly supports national energy security by reducing reliance on imported coal and conserving vital foreign exchange reserves.
Why Non Torrefied We are choosing Non-Torrefied Pellets over torrefied ones. Torrefaction involves 'roasting' biomass in the absence of oxygen to remove moisture and volatile compounds, turning it into a coal-like material. In contrast, non-torrefied pellets are simply compressed biomass; they retain volatile compounds that burn rapidly and they can easily absorb moisture. Mechanically, the technology for non-torrefied pellets is operationally simpler, proven, less capital-intensive, mature, and economically viable in India. Conversely, torrefaction technology is still in its pilot stages, commercially unproven locally, and rife with complex operational hurdles.
Other Applications of Bio Coal Pellets Apart from co-firing with coal in Power Generation Bio Coal Pellets can be used in Industrial boilers, steam generation plants, textile dyeing units, food processing industries, chemical plants, paper mills and ceramic industries.
Other Advantages of Bio Coal Pellets Bio-coal pellets offer a powerful combination of logistical flexibility, environmental remediation, and economic resilience. Operationally, they are easy to store in bulk, use on demand, and provide a stable, cost-effective alternative to volatile fossil fuels like furnace oil and coal. Environmentally, they provide a carbon-neutral green fuel that slashes greenhouse gases and industrial particulate emissions while directly mitigating the crisis of open-field stubble burning. Strategically, this localized energy model leverages aggressive Government of India renewable mandates to substitute expensive imported coal, strengthening national energy security and keeping capital within the domestic rural economy.
3. Project Scope and Product Specification
Particular Detail
Plant Capacity 2 Tons per Hour or 32 Tos per Day (with option to scale to a higher capacity later)
Plant Input Raw Material Paddy Husk, Paddy Straw, Wheat Straw and Mustard Stalk with moisture around 12%
Plant Working Days per Year 300 Days
Plant Working Hours per Day 16 Hours
Plant Shift Pattern 2 Shifts (8 Hours Each)
Total Operating Hours per Yer 4,800 Hours
Product Physical Charecteristics Uniform cylindrical shape; diameter 8 mm, or 12 mm; length ranges between 10–30 mm. Smooth surface, high density, and easy handling.
Product Moisture Content Maintained between 8%–14% to ensure efficient combustion, reduced smoke emission, and higher calorific value.
Product Calorific Value (GCV) 3,000 to 3,600 Kcal/kg
Product Bulk Density 600 to 700 kg/m3 enabling cost-effective storage and transportation.
Product Ash Content Below 12% for boiler efficiency
Product Volatile Matter Content Between 50% to 60% suitable for co-firing
Fines Below 5%
Particle size after pulverizing Mostly below 1mm, at most below 3mm
