Milestones:



1. Aquire Site - 4 bigha Land for Plant & three weeks of feedstock & Biocoal storage. Must have Electricity connection, Road connection, Water, higher ground.

2. Business Registration - Kudra Agro India Private Limited. Durgavati Agro India Private Limited. (Done)

4. GST registration.

5. MSME registration under udyam portal to be recognized as an MSME unit.

6. Register with GeM portal and enroll in biomass category to get tender updates on biomass.

7. Upload product catalogue on GeM portal and keep it updated.

8. Register with Bihar state specific eProcurement portal to get tender updates on biomass. (optional)

9. Adani, Jindal, etc have their own portal where they seek Expression of Interest to supply Biocoal. Register there as well. (Optional)

10. Get Quote from Plant Vendors for machinery and product specification. Meet Dalmia Cement, convey abilities, and incorporate their requirements in the machinery quote. Also include briquette capability in quote.

11. Get estimates for Civil construction work for shed from contractor.

12. Prepare Detailed Project Report document with total cost estimation including capital investment, construction and development, working capital etc.

13. Connect with KIP Subsidy consultant to review and update all project documents and plan.

14. Plan funding. How much we are investing in Equity, how much we need in Loan from bank. Get in principle commitment for 3 crore or more of capital in liu of equity.

15. Apply and get all statutory approvals, NOC, licenses, permits before we go to bank for loan. Land conversion, pollution CTE, CTO, State specific approvals. Approval from Ministry of Agriculture for AIF (interest subsidy). MNRE IP approval from bio urja portal of MNRE. Pollution Control Board – CTE.

16. Discuss with chosen Plant suppliers the design, layout, & specifications of the plant and ask for a final quote that we will submit to the Bank.

17. Discuss with the chosen Plant supplier, the design and specification for the Shed and factory floor, storage space etc.

18. Discuss with the chosen Plant supplier all the other tools and things that we will need to run the plant. Outside of their quote.

19. Submit Loan application to Bank. Sanction Loan. Get money in bank. Bank considers – net worth, site, business registration, DPR, approvals, civil work quote, plant quote, subsidies, etc.

20. Wait till the Loan is sanctioned and money comes in the bank. Now we have all the funds including equity and loan.

21. Discuss & Prepare terms for purchase order for the plant. Order 10 TPH (100 TPD) plant that will be supplied over several years linked with payments. First delivery and payment will be only for 2TPH or 5 TPH.

22. Place the order for the plant.

21. Apply for the NTPC and other tenders & seek Purchase Orders

22. Get 500 KVA Electricity connection and transformer.

23. Now start construction, order machinery, get raw material. Get the shed constructed, the plant installed, all other equipment purchased, Raw Material purchased.

24. Get the plant operator hired and trained. All other staff hired as well.

25. Do a trial run of the plant. If successful, start production.

26. Start claiming all the benefits and subsidies etc. Capital subsidy, interest subsidy, stamp duty refund, state specific benefits. One time and year after year.

27. Get a Quality Certification, like ISO.

28. Register with the Directorate General of Supplies and Disposals (DGS&D)

29. Enrol with the National Small Industries Corporation (NSIC)

30. Experience and get the Plant vendor to Fix all issues in the plant in next few months or an year

31. Expand our operation to collecting biomass ourselves from farms using balers.

32. Setup two separate storages on leased land near farms 50 km away.

33. Grow bamboo on leased farms and Expand to make pellets from bamboo dust and wood dust that have GVC of 4500 and sell for Rs 15 in private sector.
